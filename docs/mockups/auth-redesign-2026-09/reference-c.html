Smelter sign-in, version C

Interactive reference for the auth flow: welcome, sign-in sheet, three-step invite onboarding, and account creation on smelterpos.com inside the in-app browser. Every control on the phone works. Same Studio tokens and Glide motion as the 2.4 reference.

Controls

Walk it

  • I was invited pushes step 1. Tap Paste to drop in a join link, Continue checks it, step 2 confirms name, restaurant and role. Google or Apple finish there; Email and password adds step 3 with live password rules. X on any step returns to Welcome.
  • I have an account opens the sign-in sheet. Google and Apple are one tap. The email form enables Sign in once both fields are filled; focusing a field lifts the sheet to the 88% detent. Drag the sheet down to dismiss.
  • I'm setting up a restaurant presents smelterpos.com in the in-app browser: create the account (Google or form), name the restaurant, done. Open the app closes the browser and opens the sign-in sheet.
  • Ready pops the check ring, then See today's list crossfades into the Order tab. Sign out on the stub restarts.

Changes from the static option C

  • Welcome is the wordmark, the word Welcome, and the three doors at the bottom. No question, no subtitle.
  • Every X is the same 30pt white circle used in the sheet header.
  • The uppercase caption label is gone from auth entirely. Field labels and the "Yes, sign me in with" line use the 13pt regular ink2 style, the same voice as "or use email".
  • C7 is a working website: sign-up, restaurant setup, done, with URL changes in the Safari bar.

Motion

  • Root screens: Glide crossfade, 10pt rise, 300ms cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1).
  • Wizard steps: push from right 280ms; the layer underneath parks at -24%. Finishing fades the stack over the Ready screen.
  • Sheet: 320ms slide, scrim 240ms, drag to dismiss past 90pt.
  • In-app browser: iOS modal, 360ms rise while the app scales to 93%.
  • Check marks and the Ready ring use pop. Nothing else overshoots.
